# Medium CTF – Privilege Misuse Investigation

During a permissions review, you find that a support engineer account was recently added to the "Domain Admins" group.

Event logs show:

```
14:21  Account added to Domain Admins
14:23  Multiple admin shares accessed
14:30  Account removed from Domain Admins
```

No change ticket exists for this action.

---

## Question

What does this activity most likely indicate?

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** Normal troubleshooting procedure
- **B)** Automated patch management
- **C)** Temporary privilege escalation to hide activity
- **D)** A backup system running normally

---

Correct Flag: **C**
