# Firewall Log Analysis


Firewall log analysis is the process of reviewing the records generated by a firewall to understand what is happening on a network. Every connection attempt, blocked packet, or unusual traffic pattern can leave traces in these logs.

For defenders, logs are one of the most reliable sources of truth. They help answer simple but important questions: who connected, where from, what service was targeted, and whether the activity looks normal or suspicious.

---

## Why Firewall Logs Matter

Attackers rarely appear out of nowhere. Before a compromise, there are usually signals:

- Repeated connection attempts from unknown sources  
- Scanning activity across many ports or hosts  
- Unexpected outbound traffic  
- Sudden spikes in traffic volume  
- Connections at unusual times

Firewall logs help defenders see these patterns early, often before a full incident develops.

---

## How Attackers Show Up in Logs

Most malicious activity follows a recognizable flow:

- Reconnaissance scans looking for open services  
- Repeated connection attempts against exposed systems  
- Access attempts using common ports and protocols  
- Communication with suspicious external hosts  
- Data leaving the network after a compromise

Not every strange entry is an attack, which is why context and pattern recognition matter more than single events.

---

## What Defenders Look For

When analyzing logs, defenders usually focus on:

- Source and destination IP patterns  
- Allowed vs blocked traffic  
- Unusual protocols or ports  
- Traffic volume changes  
- Repeated failed connection attempts

The goal is to move from individual log lines to a clear story of what happened on the network.
