# Easy CTF 1 - Find the Overprivileged Account

You are reviewing Active Directory group memberships after a routine audit request.

You pull the following data:

```
User: j.harris
Department: IT Helpdesk
Groups: Domain Users, Helpdesk-Tier1, Domain Admins
Last password change: 847 days ago
```

---

## Question

What is the most critical finding in this account's configuration?

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** The account has not changed its password in over two years
- **B)** The account belongs to too many groups
- **C)** A Helpdesk Tier 1 employee is a member of Domain Admins
- **D)** The account is part of the Domain Users group

---

Correct Flag: **C**
