## Suspicious Email Analysis

A user reports an email that looks like it came from internal IT support.

```
From: it-support@company-helpdesk.com
Subject: Urgent Password Reset Required

Hello,
Your account will be disabled in 30 minutes unless you verify it now.
Click here: http://company-security-check.com/login
```

The user says they do not recognize the sender domain.
