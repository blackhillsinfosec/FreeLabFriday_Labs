# Medium CTF – Credential Abuse

A user reports unusual logins to their cloud account shortly after clicking a link in an email.

You are asked to review authentication logs.

---

## Observation

Logs show:

- Successful login from a foreign country  
- Login happened 3 minutes after the phishing email was opened  
- No MFA challenge was triggered  
- The account owner confirms they never traveled

---

## Question

What **most likely** happened?

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** Password brute-force attack  
- **B)** Stolen session cookie  
- **C)** Credentials were harvested via phishing  
- **D)** Exploited cloud vulnerability  

---

Correct Flag: **C**
