# Medium CTF - Credential Abuse Investigation

After a phishing campaign, a security analyst reviews authentication logs.

They observe:

- A successful login from a new country
- The login used valid credentials on the first attempt
- Access occurred outside normal working hours
- No malware alerts were triggered on the endpoint

---

## Question

What is the MOST likely explanation?

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** The attacker stole credentials through social engineering
- **B)** The account password expired
- **C)** A vulnerability scanner triggered a false login
- **D)** The user forgot they were traveling

---

Correct Flag: **A**
