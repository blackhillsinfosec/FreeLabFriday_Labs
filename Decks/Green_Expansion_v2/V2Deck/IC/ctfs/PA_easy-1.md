# Easy CTF 1 - Badge Clone Basics

A security guard notices a man standing near the office entrance for about 10 minutes. He holds his phone close to employees as they badge in, never actually entering the building himself. The next morning, someone enters the server room at 2:14 AM using a valid badge belonging to a developer who was on vacation.

---

## Question

What technique did the attacker most likely use to gain access?

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** He guessed the door PIN by watching employees type it
- **B)** He cloned an employee's RFID badge using a hidden reader
- **C)** He picked the lock on the server room door
- **D)** He social engineered the guard into letting him in

---

Correct Flag: **B**
