# Medium CTF - Impersonation Investigation

Your company's receptionist receives a call in the morning from someone claiming to be from the building's HVAC maintenance company. They say a technician will be stopping by to check the cooling units in the server room. No scheduled maintenance is on record.

At 11:30 AM, a man arrives wearing a uniform, carrying a clipboard and a toolbox, and presents a work ID badge. The receptionist lets him through. He spends 22 minutes in the server room alone.

Two weeks later, forensics finds a network tap installed behind one of the rack units, passively capturing all unencrypted traffic on that segment.

---

## Question

Which combination of tools and techniques made this attack successful? Select the most complete and accurate answer.

---

## Flags (Choose One)

- **A)** The attacker used a crowbar to force entry, then installed malware on a workstation
- **B)** The attacker used a counterfeit work ID and a clipboard to impersonate a technician, gaining unescorted access to plant a network tap
- **C)** The attacker cloned an employee badge and used it to access the server room during off-hours
- **D)** The attacker bribed the receptionist to look away while they bypassed the badge reader

---

Correct Flag: **B**
