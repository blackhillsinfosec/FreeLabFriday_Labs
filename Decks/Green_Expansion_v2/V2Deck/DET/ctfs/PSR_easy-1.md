## Badge and Entry Basics

A security reviewer is assessing a mid-sized office. During the walkthrough, they notice that the front entrance uses RFID badge readers. They borrow a colleague's badge to test access.

When they hold the badge near the reader, the door unlocks immediately. They also notice the door stays open for about 8 seconds before closing.

Later, they check the badge management system and find 14 accounts belonging to employees who left the company over the past year. All 14 badges are still marked as active.
