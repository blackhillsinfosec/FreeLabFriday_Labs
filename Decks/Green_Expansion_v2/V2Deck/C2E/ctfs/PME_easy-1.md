## Spot the Device

A red teamer walks into the target company's office posing as an IT contractor. He is given a brief tour of the server room. While the guide steps out to take a call, he plugs a small device into an open USB port on one of the servers and lets it run for two minutes. He then unplugs it and leaves.

No alarms fire. No one stops him.
